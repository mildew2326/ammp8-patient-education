Simple patient guide · aMMP-8 saliva test

What is this mouth test, and why does it matter?

We use a quick saliva / mouth-rinse screen to check whether your gums are actively breaking down right now — and what that may mean for your whole-body health.

In plain words: this test looks for a “breakdown signal” made by your immune system when gum tissue is under attack.
1

Your immune system responds

When bacteria bother your gums, your body sends helpers to fight them. That is normal and good.

2

Sometimes the fight gets too strong

If the reaction stays too active, it can start breaking down the tissues that hold your teeth.

3

We measure that signal

The test checks for aMMP-8, a protein linked to active tissue breakdown — in real time.

What aMMP-8 means

aMMP-8 is a natural enzyme your body can make when inflammation is actively damaging tissue.

  • It is not a test for one germ.
  • It is not the same as “do I have cavities?”
  • It helps show if gum damage is happening now.
  • It helps us track whether treatment is working.

What your result may look like

Lower

Usually a calmer immune response. We still protect your gums with good care and checkups.

Raised

More active inflammation. We may recommend deeper cleaning, closer follow-up, and home-care changes.

Very high

Strong active breakdown signal. We act on gum health and may also discuss whole-body inflammation risk.

Mouth health ↔ body health

Mouth Active gum inflammation and tissue stress
↔
Body Shared inflammatory pathways (including diabetes risk links)

Your mouth and body share the same immune system. Long-lasting gum inflammation can add to overall inflammatory stress. That is why very high results may lead us to coordinate with your primary care provider.

What this means for you

This screening helps us see if your gums are quietly under attack before more damage builds up. If your number is high, we don’t panic — we make a plan: treat the gums, re-check the signal, and if needed, connect care with your medical doctor for whole-body health.
